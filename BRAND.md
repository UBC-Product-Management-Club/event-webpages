# Product Jackpot: brand notes (for humans and AI tools)

Vegas casino meets product management. Dark, bold, neon, playful. Always match the Instagram designs.

## Colours
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| periwinkle | `#8D9BEB` | primary, neon accents, gradient start |
| mauve | `#AF71AA` | primary, gradient middle |
| signal red | `#E33148` | primary, neon red, CTAs, hearts/diamonds |
| charcoal | `#2E2E2E` | secondary, dark UI |
| indigo | `#445088` | secondary, navy chips |
| mist | `#DEDEE2` | secondary, light text/cards |
| maroon bg | `#1A0000` to `#3D0000` | page background (from the Instagram posts) |
| gold | `#FFD9A8` / `#E8A54B` / `#A8651D` | roulette-style gradient: lines, frames, labels |

Brand gradient (headings like "Colour Palette"): `#8D9BEB -> #AF71AA -> #E33148`.

## Type
- Poppins (700-900, uppercase, tight) for headlines and UI.
- Poltawski Nowy for anything that lives on a playing card.

## Motifs
- Gear = PMC. The four suits are redrawn as angular shapes. Hearts/diamonds are red, spades/clubs are navy.
- Dealer mascot "Geary" (two expressions: happy, smirk).
- Chips, roulette wheel, card backs (purple/red with gold gear line art), damask pattern, thin gold vertical lines, spotlight beam.

## Copy lines from the campaign
"Do you have the winning hand?" / "Ready to hit the jackpot?"

## Motion
Floating chips/cards, slowly spinning roulette, slot reels landing on 7-7-7, cards flipping to reveal content, neon flicker, marquee bulbs. Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
